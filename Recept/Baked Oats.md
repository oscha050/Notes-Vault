# Baked Oats Meal Prep (3–4 Portions)

## Ingredients (Base)
- 300g oats  
- 4 eggs  
- 500 ml milk  
- 2 bananas (mashed)  
- 3–4 tbsp peanut butter  
- 2 tsp baking powder  
- 1 tsp salt  
### Optional
- Cinnamon or vanilla  

---
## Method
1. Preheat oven to 180°C  
2. Mix all ingredients thoroughly  
3. Pour into a lined or greased baking dish  
4. Bake for 25–30 minutes  
5. Let cool completely before cutting  

Cut into 3–4 equal portions  

---
## Storage
- Store in fridge up to 4 days  
- Keep toppings separate  

---
## Reheating
- Microwave 1–2 minutes  
- Add a splash of milk if dry  

---
# Daily Assembly

## Base (per serving)
- 1 portion baked oats  
## Toppings
- 2–3 tbsp kvarg  
- Handful berries (raspberries, blueberries)  
- Small amount mango (optional)  
- 1 tbsp toasted hazelnuts  
### Optional finish
- Drizzle honey  
- Pinch of salt  

---
# Taste Variations

## 1. Berry Fresh
- Kvarg  
- Raspberries + blueberries  
- Hazelnuts  
- Small honey drizzle  

---
## 2. Apple Cinnamon
- Lightly sautéed apple pieces  
- Cinnamon  
- Walnuts  
- Kvarg or yogurt  

---
## 3. Chocolate Peanut
- Add cocoa powder to base  
- Banana slices  
- Peanut butter drizzle  
- Optional dark chocolate  

---
## 4. Tropical
- Mango  
- Coconut flakes  
- Kvarg  
- Lime zest  

---
## 5. High Protein
- Add protein powder to base (reduce oats slightly)  
- Kvarg topping  
- Nuts/seeds  

---
# Notes
- Do not overload with toppings  
- Always include a pinch of salt  
- Toast nuts for better flavor  
- Rotate variations to avoid boredom
